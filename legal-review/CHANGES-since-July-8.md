# Lendie Terms of Service — changes since the July 8, 2026 draft

Updated draft: **Last Updated November 20, 2026** (`Lendie-Terms-of-Service.docx`).
The Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy have **not** changed since July 8.

All changes concern card payments, which are built but **not yet switched on**. The app runs
cash-only today, so nothing below is in effect for users yet.

## §6.3 Owner Payouts — two new paragraphs
1. **Payment hold (buyer protection).** Card payments are held in our Stripe platform balance
   before the owner/provider is paid:
   - dated rentals/services: released ~24h after the last day of the booking;
   - purchases and undated services: released 24h after the buyer confirms receipt, or
     72h after the seller marks it handed over (the buyer is notified), whichever comes
     first. If neither happens, it's released 14 days after payment. The clock starts at
     the handoff, not at payment, because local pickups can be days after paying.
   - States that the card is charged in full and the money is held in our platform
     account. It is not a temporary authorization hold on the card.
2. **Reporting a problem.** Either party can report a problem until the payout releases,
   with the exact deadlines spelled out. A renter can report once a dated booking has
   started; before that they cancel instead. A report
   freezes the payout until Lendie reviews it and decides to release, refund in full, or
   partially refund (deducted from the held payout). The clause says our review is not
   arbitration and doesn't limit chargebacks or legal remedies.

   **Questions for you:** see ATTORNEY_BRIEF item **7b**. Does holding funds this long and
   deciding disputes create money-transmitter or escrow-licensing exposure? Is the
   "not arbitration" wording adequate?

## §6.4 Cancellation Policy — three edits
- **No refund after a rental starts.** The existing "no refund after the start time"
  sentence is now enforced by the app.
- **New sentence on purchases and undated services.** The buyer can cancel for a refund
  (less the 8% service fee) only within 24h of payment and before confirming receipt. After
  that, problems go through "Report a problem" while the payment is held.
- **Contradiction fixed.** "Service fees are non-refundable" now reads "…except when the
  Owner or Provider cancels." The earlier paragraph already promised a full refund, fee
  included, when the owner cancels.

## Other wording added (Nov 20, after a second review)
- **§6.1:** says what the fee is charged on: 8% of the rental, service or item price,
  excluding delivery and taxes, shown as its own line at checkout; 4% deducted from the
  owner's payout.
- **§6.4, cash:** Lendie can still act on fraud or misconduct, and suspend accounts,
  whatever the payment method.
- **§6.3, start date:** a dated booking starts on its first booked day. A renter can
  report the owner not handing the item over.
- **§6.4, "no refunds after start":** now says this doesn't limit refunds granted through
  an approved problem report (owner no-show, item unusable or not as described).
- **§6.4, timing:** refunds "typically" take 5–10 business days; we no longer promise a
  bank's processing time.

## Disclosure at checkout (for consistency review)
- **Purchases:** "Lendie holds your payment until you have the item: the seller is paid 24
  hours after you confirm you received it, or 72 hours after they mark it handed over. You
  can report a problem until then, and cancel within 24 hours of paying for a refund, minus
  the non-refundable 8% service fee."
- **Rentals/services:** "Lendie holds your payment until 24 hours after the booking ends, and
  you can report a problem until then. You can cancel for a refund, minus the non-refundable 8% service
  fee, until the booking starts (or within 24 hours of payment if it has no date)."
